For you, my love

There is something I need to say.

My dearest,

I am sorry. Those two words are small, and what I feel behind them is not. I hurt you, and nothing is heavier to me than knowing that.

You deserve gentleness, patience, and a man who listens before he speaks. I was not that, and I am sorry for it. I would take back the moment if I could. Since I cannot, I will spend every day after it being better.

You are the best part of my days. Loving you is the one thing I have never had to think about, and that is why I never want to give you a reason to doubt it.

Please forgive me. Let me hold your hand again and show you, not just tell you, that you are cherished.

Forever yours,Your love
